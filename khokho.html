WHY PLAY KHO-KHO?

Kho-Kho is a fast-paced traditional Indian team sport that develops speed, agility, stamina, reflexes, and quick decision-making. It involves rapid chasing, dodging, and changing directions, helping improve body coordination and flexibility. The sport encourages teamwork, discipline, strategic thinking, and physical fitness while providing an energetic and enjoyable experience.
